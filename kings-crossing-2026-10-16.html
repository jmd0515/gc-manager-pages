Where to focus

Shorter weekday waits

19.6% of customers waited 15+ minutes
That's about 1 in 5 customers, against 12.7% across the market, and the average wait was 8.3 minutes. With weekends down to 10%, the long waits are happening Monday through Friday. Long waits are when customers walk out or try another salon next time.
  • Find the weekday peaks. Pull the Peak Coverage report and look for the weekday half-hours that need 3 or more stylists but are short-staffed.
  • Move hours, don't add them. Shift a slow-hour shift into those peaks, the same way the weekend fix worked.
  • Keep cuts moving. Average haircut time is 16.5 minutes against 14.8 in the market. Shaving a minute off each cut frees up a lot of chair time on a busy afternoon.
