Where to focus

Keep cutting the waits

16.7% of customers waited 15+ minutes
Better than 19.6% last week, but still #75 of 92 against 11.4% in the market. Two things are driving it. Haircuts average 16.4 minutes against 14.9 in the market, which adds up to about 10 hours of chair time a week. And weekend hours slipped to 22.5% of the schedule (#88 of 91), so weekend waits went back up to 12.8%.
  • Keep cuts moving. Coach anyone running well over 16 minutes back toward 14–15.
  • Protect weekend hours. Get back to at least a quarter of hours on Sat/Sun, where you were last week.
  • Use the Peak Coverage report to move slow weekday shifts into the peaks.
