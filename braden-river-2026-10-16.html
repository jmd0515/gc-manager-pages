Where to focus

Keep waits short as you grow

14.3% of customers waited 15+ minutes
Up from about 11% in September, and now above the market (12.7%). Haircut time has crept up too, from about 14.9 minutes in the spring to 15.7 over the last three weeks, against 14.8 in the market. More customers plus longer cuts means longer waits, and long waits are how a growing salon loses the customers it just won.
  • Watch haircut time. Coach anyone running well over 16 minutes back toward 14–15. A minute saved on every cut is about 8 hours of chair time a week.
  • Cover the peaks. Check the Peak Coverage report for half-hours that need a third stylist and move a shift there.
  • Push Online Check-In. Customers who check in online wait at home instead of in the lobby, so a busy afternoon feels shorter to them.
